SOURCE_MANIFEST.json records the source commit, original paths and changes made for this standalone export. No original repository, branch, visibility or history was modified for publication.

No root LICENSE file was present in the supplied project/runtime snapshot. This export does not invent or replace an upstream license grant. Third-party Python and npm dependencies retain their respective licenses. Paper training data remains at its original public source and is downloaded only on request; its original terms apply.

No FlowLab application/backend, Lab edge, hardware drivers, copied FlowLab screens, proprietary equipment images, calibrated equipment-instance registry, credentials, raw paper PDFs, spreadsheets or training datasets are included. The six reproduction-specific React components are XP Plus screens originally added in the Hackathon parent repository; the full copied FlowLab frontend is excluded.
