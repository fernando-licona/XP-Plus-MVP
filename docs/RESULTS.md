# Computational findings

These are recorded scientific comparisons, not new physical laboratory measurements.

## Delgado-Licona et al. (2025)

- Explicitly including the 228 uL detector line adds about 1.8 minutes of arrival delay at the nominal 9-minute residence-time condition. Omitting that line gives closer agreement with the paper's reported mean residence time. Whether the original analysis corrected for the line needs confirmation.
- The model reconstruction reports CV R2 about 0.9855 for emission wavelength and 0.9661 for FWHM, compared with the paper's 0.967 and 0.988. The apparent label interchange is a question for the authors, not an established error.
- Seven steady-state-equivalent emission predictions agree within the paper's 4.1 nm comparison criterion. Comparison against the training ramp is not independent validation.
- The exact experiment descriptions and provenance-tagged assumptions are under `examples/dfe_rtd/` and `examples/dfe_gp/`. The Standard is regenerated by the public pipeline; no precomputed receipt is used as a numerical substitute.

## Junbin Li / PoLARIS (2026)

Checked with the supplied main paper, supplement and 24-sheet workbook and the authors' verified Zenodo deposit. The initialization CSV has 80 rows; the seven GP inputs exclude PFO, matching the deposited script. Cross-validation uses 10 shuffled folds, split seed 42, original CSV order and float64.

| Check | R2 |
| --- | ---: |
| Workbook Figure 4a, A3:B82 | 0.7085807109 |
| Current BoTorch defaults | 0.7400955893 |
| Legacy-default backend | 0.7146160767 |

The authors' inspected CV function and XP Plus's current-default implementation produced identical predictions in the tested environment. Legacy defaults recover the rounded reported 0.71, but do not reproduce every workbook prediction exactly. The original dependency versions and model defaults remain unconfirmed. A fixed-condition GP was also wired through the actual black-box engine on explicitly synthetic transport; this does not validate the real paper rig.

Additional workbook checks: zero-intercept PLQY calibration coefficient 8.0201747085e-6; champion stability CV about 3.95%. These recalculate published evidence and do not supply the physical-results column.

The optional scripts in `examples/polaris_gp/` require separately obtained, hash-matched `SourceData.xlsx` and the deposited `Initialization_Campaign.csv`. No raw PDF/XLSX/CSV is bundled. The CSV SHA256 is `113aa4853fb7a408a8707c6f73853b2db3754c2829779486c58eb7a967bf1dca`.

Remaining limits: original software identity, accepted automatic extraction, established hydraulic/optical details, dynamic absolute-flow bindings, segmented-flow/PLQY qualification and actual physical reproduction. The public demo does not close those gaps.
